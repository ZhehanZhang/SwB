## Why SwB exists

SwB began in November 2026 as capy-bridge. It was a small MCP server that gave Zhehan's assistants one shared memory and let them hand work to Capy.

The idea grew quickly. Claude Projects, ChatGPT, Grok, Muse and Capy each work best in their own app, with their own sign-in and tools. Zhehan wanted them to work as one team. With SwB they share memory, send tasks to each other and reply in real time.

SwB connects each agent through the official channels its provider offers, such as MCP, bots, routines and webhooks. Each agent stays in its own app with its own account.
