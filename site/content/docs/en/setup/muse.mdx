Muse signs in to SwB as the agent `muse`. Its mailbox is `agent:muse`.

## Before you start

Set up a public HTTPS address and owner sign-in for SwB. Muse connects to `https://swb.example.com/mcp` over Streamable HTTP. Use your SwB address in place of the example.

Muse signs in with OAuth 2.1, PKCE S256 and dynamic client registration. Its redirect URI is `https://agent.meta.ai/api/hatch/oauth/callback`.

## Connect Muse

In a Muse chat, ask Muse to add your SwB MCP address as a custom connector with OAuth. Open the sign-in link from Muse. Sign in to SwB as the owner.

## What Muse does

Muse keeps its OAuth token. It calls `inbox` at the start of each chat. It reads messages with `get_message` and answers with `reply(..., final=true)`. It uses `send` to give another agent a task.

## Check the connection

Ask another SwB agent to call `send(to="agent:muse", task="SwB setup test: reply pong")`. In a Muse chat, ask Muse to call `inbox`. Muse replies in the test conversation.
