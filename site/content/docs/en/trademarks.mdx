ZhehanZ reserves the SwB name, the `swb-one` package name, and the SwB marks and logo.

A fork is a separate copy of a project. Use a statement like this for a separate project, fork or service. If ZhehanZ gives written permission, follow its terms instead.

> This project is separate from SwB. It is not affiliated with SwB and has no endorsement from SwB.

You can make factual references such as “works with SwB”.

We welcome contributions to SwB and prefer them over forks. Give your fork a name and logo distinct from SwB’s. Keep `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`. Give credit with a link such as “[Based on SwB](https://github.com/ZhehanZhang/SwB)”.

Ask ZhehanZ for written permission at [hello@swb.one](mailto:hello@swb.one). See the [SwB trademark policy](https://github.com/ZhehanZhang/SwB/blob/release/1.0/TRADEMARKS.md) for the license terms.
